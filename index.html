01 — UNDERSTANDING AI

What is an AI Agent?

An AI Agent is an intelligent system that can understand a goal, make decisions, use tools and take actions to complete a task.

🧠

UNDERSTAND

Understand the user's goal and requirements.

💡

REASON

Decide the best approach to solve the task.

🛠️

USE TOOLS

Use data, APIs and external tools when required.

⚡

TAKE ACTION

Execute the task and provide a useful result.
